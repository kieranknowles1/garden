# The AI Bubble
[date::2025-09-21]
I consider the current AI trend to be a bubble, big tech is rushing to build the most capable LLM and to slap gen AI into everything, even when it makes no sense. It seems like current research is to throw more resources at the problem with no regard for efficiency. I believe that AI can be useful - Immich uses it excellently to classify images and Paperless to scan and categorize documents (though I've set most tags and correspondents to be based solely on document contents). Even gen AI is good for analyzing text or an advanced autocomplete for code. But the kinds of AI that tech companies shove in your face are not this, and it seems guaranteed that models will consume themselves with the amount of AI slop that is being churned out of the sewer.

Overall, it seems that the "innovation parrot" and line go up economics is forcing all big companies to copy each other to avoid being seen as left behind by their shareholders. Even chess.com has boarded the train with completely unnecessary use of TTS to voice game reviews.
