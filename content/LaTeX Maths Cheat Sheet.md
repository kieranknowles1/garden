# Tex Maths Cheat Sheet
Cheat sheet for common LaTeX maths expressions.

See also: [Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/learn/latex/Mathematical_expressions)
# Fractions
$$\frac{1}{2}$$
```tex
\frac{1}{2}
```
# Matrices
$$
\begin{bmatrix}
	1 & 2 & 3 & 4 \\
	a & b & c & d \\
	9 & 8 & 7 & 6 \\
	e & f & g & h \\
\end{bmatrix}
$$
```tex
\begin{bmatrix}
	1 & 2 & 3 & 4 \\
	a & b & c & d \\
	9 & 8 & 7 & 6 \\
	e & f & g & h \\
\end{bmatrix}
```
# Normalised Vector
$$\hat{x}$$
```tex
\hat{x}
```
# Symbols

$$\cdot$$
```tex
\cdot
```

$$\times$$
```tex
\times
```
## Logical Operators, AND, OR, NOT
$$\land \lor \lnot$$
```tex
\land \lor \lnot
```
## Not Equals
$$\neq$$
```tex
\neq
```
